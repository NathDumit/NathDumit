Definitions. Sales = US$ value of active bookings by check-in year and month. Gross margin = 1 − cost ÷ sales, on invoiced services only. Room nights = nights × rooms (hotel only). ADR = hotel sales ÷ room nights. YTD = 1 January to the cut-off date. STLY = today's book against the book on the same date last year. Forecast = book + historical pickup; a simple projection that does not replace commercial analysis. HHI from 0 to 10,000: above 2,500 indicates concentration. Synthetic data; methodology inspired by real revenue-management routines, with no company data.
